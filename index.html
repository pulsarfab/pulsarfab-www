Hardware · in development

Built around an open observatory.

Open firmware, open PCBs, and standard astronomy protocols. These hardware projects are still being developed.

pulsardew

USB dew heater controller

A compact controller built on STM32G0 with STM32 HAL and FreeRTOS. Keep optics clear from a laptop or USB hub.

In development

pulsardewpro

WiFi dew control

An ESP32-S3 controller speaking ASCOM Alpaca, for network-connected dew control.

In development

crunchdefender

Mount limit guard

An STM32F405 USB host/device design for Sky-Watcher mounts, intended to interrupt motion before a collision.
